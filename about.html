About This Blog

AI engineering, LLMs, and real-world backend systems.

Welcome to Elvin's AI engineering notes, a developer-focused space where I explore how to build real software with large language models: agents and tool use, retrieval and RAG, evals, inference cost and latency, and the backend systems that hold it all together.

Most posts look at one design decision from an engineer's point of view: what the options are, where each one wins, and what it costs. Expect diagrams, trade-off tables, and draft designs rather than hype.

Who am I?

I'm Elvin Jamalov, a software engineer focused on backend development, cloud systems, and building software with AI.
